# 6. Progression — Funding & Upgrades

## 6.1 Currencies

### Reputation ⭐
- **Earned from:** completed samples, published papers (delayed, planned), successful proposals
- **Determines:** yearly funding grant amount, available proposal quality, Source Upgrade eligibility
- **Lost from:** unfulfilled committed proposals (−8 per proposal with 0 samples)

### Funding 💰
- **Starting amount:** 200K per run
- **Year-end grant:** 200K + (reputation × 500)
- **Carryover cap:** max 100K can carry from year to year (use it or lose most of it)
- **Postdoc salary:** −100K per postdoc deducted from the year-end total (after grant + carryover)
- Spent in the upgrade shop between cycles. Cannot be earned during gameplay — only via grant.

## 6.2 Upgrade Shop (Cycle End)

All upgrades are **persistent across cycles and years**. Purchased upgrades show ✓ OWNED and cannot be repurchased (except stackable ones).

Year 1 is a tutorial run — the shop is limited to accessible, easy-to-understand upgrades. More structural upgrades unlock from year 2 onwards.

The shop has two tabs: **🧪 Lab** (the upgrades below) and **🤖 Robots** (the automation path, §6.6). The Robots tab appears from the first cycle end.

### Unlock Schedule

| Upgrade | Available from | Effect | Cost | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Faster Prep — Wet Lab 🧪 | Y1C1 | Prep time −5% per purchase for the Wet Lab only (max 10×, −50% total). **Once per cycle end screen.** | 💰 10K | 10× |
| Faster Prep — Dry Lab 🔩 | Y1C1 | Prep time −5% per purchase for the Dry Lab only (max 10×, −50% total). **Once per cycle end screen.** | 💰 10K | 10× |

User Coordination and Predictive Maintenance were cut for the robot path: Reception Bots and Hutch Bots do their jobs.

## 6.4 Postdoc Intelligence Levels

Postdocs use a **level-based AI system** that improves with upgrades. Each postdoc has three states: `idle`, `moving`, `working`. Levels are **per-postdoc** — hiring a second postdoc starts them at Level 1 regardless of the first postdoc's level.

### Level 1 — Follow & Takeover *(current implementation)*

| Behaviour | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Default** | Random wandering inside the prep ring |
| **Trigger** | Player enters a room AND starts an action (deposit sample, begin experiment setup, start measurement) |
| **Response** | Postdoc walks to the room at player speed |
| **Takeover** | Once arrived, postdoc "takes over" — the action continues even after the player leaves the room |
| **Scope** | Prep rooms, Experimental Hutch (experiment setup), Control Room (measurement) |
| **Limitation** | Cannot initiate actions — only takes over player-started ones |

Only one postdoc is assigned per room. Labels: `idle` → `▶ helping` → `⚙ working`.

### Level 2 — Autonomous *(implemented)*

| Behaviour | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Default** | Random wandering (same as Level 1) |
| **Trigger** | Any active prep slot or started measurement — **no player presence required** |
| **Response** | Postdoc walks to the station autonomously |
| **Takeover** | Keeps the action ticking (same as Level 1) |
| **Scope** | Prep rooms, Control Room. Experiment setup still requires player to initiate |
| **Unlocked via** | Between-cycle action: "🎓 Train Postdoc" (see §6.5) |

### Level 3 — Auto-collect *(implemented)*

| Behaviour | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A measurement the postdoc is attending finishes (timer hits zero) |
| **Action** | Postdoc automatically collects the result — no player visit required |
| **Flash** | Shows `⚙ <Name>: 🎉 <job> complete!` or `⚙ <Name>: <job> X/Y done` |
| **Scope** | Control Room measurements only |
| **Unlocked via** | Between-cycle action: "🎓 Train Postdoc" Lv2→3 (see §6.5) |

The auto-collect fires at the moment the measurement timer hits zero, collected in a deferred list to avoid mutating the slot array mid-iteration.

### Level 4+ — *(future design space)*

Potential: multi-step task chaining, sample pickup/delivery, cross-beamline coordination.

## 6.5 Between-Cycle Actions

At the end of each cycle, the player must choose **one free action** before continuing. The Continue button is locked until an action is selected.

| Action | Effect | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| 📄 Write Paper | +reputation (`10 + samples_done × 2`, capped at 30) | Always |
| 🎓 Train Postdoc | Upgrades one postdoc's AI level (1→2→3). If multiple postdocs are trainable, a sub-panel appears to choose which one. Max level is 3. | Requires at least one postdoc below max level |

This is separate from the funding-based upgrade shop — no cost, purely a strategic choice.


## 6.6 Robot Path — Full Automation by Year 5 *(Implemented)*

The long goal of a run: replace every job the player does with a robot, one station at a time, until the facility runs itself. A perfect player gets there in **Year 5 Cycle 3** at the earliest; anyone else keeps playing past Year 5 until they do. The game never ends on its own.

### Robots (🤖 Robots tab)

"Available from" is the first cycle the robot can work in; it is sold in the shop at the end of the cycle before.

| Robot | Available from | What it does | Cost | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prep Bot | Y1C2 | Parks at a lab (Wet first, then Dry); prep runs with nobody there | 💰 60K | 2 |
| Console Bot | Y2C1 | Parks in a control room (BL-1 first); starts, runs and collects scans | 💰 90K | 4 |
| Hutch Bot | Y3C1 | Parks in a hutch; takes prepped samples (rack of 2), does the experiment setup, loads the beamline, realigns motor slips | 💰 80K | 4 |
| Courier Bot | Y3C3 | Walks prepped samples from the labs to Hutch Bots | 💰 140K | 2 |
| Sample Changer | Y4C1 | Optional: every beamline holds 2 samples | 💰 200K | 1 |
| Reception Bot | Y4C2 | Collects samples from waiting users and starts them in a lab | 💰 150K | 2 |
| Facility OS | Y5C1–C3 | Accepts proposals and brings in users. Needs every robot above (except the Sample Changer). Installed in 3 stages of 💰 400K, one per shop; the last stage is sold after Y5C2 | 💰 400K × 3 | 1 |

Robots walk at 160 px/s (the player walks at 172) and avoid the ring the same way postdocs do. Robots break down now and then from Year 3 (§5.4).

The stages let a player who is behind spread the Facility OS across years, since funding over the 100K carryover cap is lost at year end.

### Victory lap

The cycle after the last Facility OS stage is bought is the **victory lap**: every task is done by robots, hazards are off, robots don't break down, and the player can just watch. A banner says so in the cycle, and the cycle-end screen shows a 🏆 Fully Automated finale with **Keep running** (carry on, still automated) and **New game**. The finale shows once.

### Timing check (simulator, Nov 2026)

| Player (sim preset) | First fully automated cycle |
|---|---|
| Perfect (`perfect`, 10 runs) | Y5C3 in 10 of 10 |
| Typical (`typical`, 10 runs) | Y5C3 in 1, Y6C1 in 5, Y6C2 in 4 |
| Weaker, robots first (8 runs) | Y6C2 in 4, Y6C3 in 4 |
| Weaker, lab upgrades and 2 postdocs first (`weaker`, 8 runs × 12 years) | Y11C3–Y12C3 in 6; not by Y12 in 2 |

Once Courier and Reception Bots arrive (around Year 4) a cycle's samples roughly double, so reputation and grants grow quickly after that.

### Postdocs and robots

Postdocs stay useful until the robots arrive and are capped at 2. A Level 3 postdoc reboots broken robots.
